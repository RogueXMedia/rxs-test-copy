Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

RogueX Services ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on roguexservices.com and how we use it.

1. Information you send us

When you use our contact form we collect your name, phone number, optional email address, and project details. We use this only to respond to you, give you a quote, and do the work you request.

2. Visitor statistics

Our site uses a simple visitor counter. Your browser stores a random visitor ID in local storage. We record the page you view, the date, the general type of device (mobile, tablet, or desktop), how you arrived (for example from Google or Facebook), and your approximate city and region. To estimate location, your browser contacts ipapi.co, which sees your IP address. Counts are stored in Google Firebase. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

3. Service providers

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers contact form messages to our email.
  • Google Firebase stores the visitor statistics described above.
  • ipapi.co provides approximate location from IP address.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts on this site.
  • HostGator, our web host, keeps standard server logs.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset the visitor ID. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you sent us.
