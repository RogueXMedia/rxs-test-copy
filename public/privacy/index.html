How this directory handles your information.

Browsing the directory

You don't need an account to browse RogueXServices.com. The directory itself does not ask for your name, phone number or email just to search. Basic technical request information may be handled by our hosting provider to deliver and protect the website.

External business and review links

Partner websites, review services and your email provider are separate services. When you use those links, their own privacy policies apply. Opening a phone link or visiting a partner's website does not create a booking or guarantee a discount.

Cookies and embedded content

The directory does not use advertising cookies or require a tracking profile to browse. Hosting and preview infrastructure may use technical features to operate the site. The directory logo is hosted as a static image; partner sites and review platforms are opened as links, not embedded videos or interactive review widgets.

Corrections and removal requests

To ask about your information or request a correction to a listing, contact hello@roguexservices.com. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant email or business listing.

Changes to this page

If the way we collect or use information changes, we will update this page to reflect the actual process.
